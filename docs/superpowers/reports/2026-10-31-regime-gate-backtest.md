## Setup
- Trade: one short strangle per UTC hour at :00, delta 0.125, 100 lots per leg, stop 2x on each leg's entry bid (a stop on either leg closes both), no take-profit, closed at the earlier of the stop and the next 12:00 UTC roll (at most 96 15m bars). Entries with fewer than 12 bars (3 hours) to the roll are skipped, so UTC 10:00 and 11:00 never trade (3572 entry hours skipped). Expiry index 1 (Next) and 2 (N2N).
- Pricing (copied from `strangle_final_5y.ts`): IV proxy = DVOL x K, flat across strikes, with K = 0.8811 calibrated as in that harness (median of real archived N2N IV / DVOL at 04:30 UTC, 2025-10-05 to 2026-10-17). Black-Scholes on Binance 15m bars, half-spread 1.2% (min $1), Delta fees + GST, portfolio margin from `portfolioMargin`.
- Periods: TRAIN 2021-11-30 to 2024-04-25, VALID to 2025-10-25, HOLDOUT to 2026-10-20 (inclusive), by entry time. Same cut points as `strangle_final_5y.ts`.
- Gate inputs at entry time te, with no lookahead: hour bars built from the 15m cache, using only hours that ended at or before te; the newest 9300 of them go to `regimeState(bars, te)` (ADX, killer) and `hourReturns(bars, te)`; RV24 = `rvWindow(rets, te, 24)`, RV7 = `rvWindow(rets, te, 168)`; IV = DVOL x K / 100; IV/RV via `ivOverRv`. DVOL is the close of the newest 1h DVOL candle that closed at or before te (null if that close is older than 6h). "DVOL < 65" compares raw DVOL points (not DVOL x K). The regime state is computed once per entry hour and shared by both expiries.
- Gates: `none`; `spec` = IV/RV24 > 1.3 AND ADX < 15 AND not killer AND DVOL < 65; `spec-RV7` = same with IV/RV7; `study` = IV/RV24 > 1.0 AND ADX < 40 AND not killer AND DVOL < 65; `spec-no-ADX15` = IV/RV24 > 1.3 AND ADX < 25 AND not killer AND DVOL < 65. A gate passes only when all its inputs are available; an unavailable input fails the gate and is counted in "Unavail".

### How to read the tables
- **Entries**: hourly entries in the period that had a valid price path for that expiry (entry hours with no path, mostly DVOL holes, are not counted; see Limits). **Passed**: cohorts (entries) the gate allowed. **Rolls**: distinct 12:00 UTC rolls that close at least one passed cohort. **Pass %** = Passed / Entries. **Unavail**: entries the gate failed because an input was missing.
- **Mean/cohort**: mean net P&L per passed cohort (USD, 100 lots per leg). **% of margin** = mean P&L / mean portfolio margin of the passed cohorts. **Total (cohort-weighted)** = sum over passed cohorts; it grows with the number of cohorts, so it is not comparable across gates with different trade counts. **Win** = share with P&L > 0. **Stop** = share closed by the 2x stop.
- **Roll-mean**: every cohort is closed at (or stopped before) one 12:00 UTC roll, and cohorts sharing a roll share most of their price path. So the passed cohorts of each roll are averaged first, and those roll averages are then averaged. It is the P&L of a typical traded roll, whatever the number of hours that passed. **SE (roll)**: standard error of the roll-mean across rolls (roll-clustered). **Roll-mean/SE** is a rough t-value. The roll-mean is the main figure for comparing gates; the per-cohort mean is shown second.
- **THIN**: fewer than 120 passed cohorts in the period. **LOW-ROLLS**: fewer than 30 distinct rolls in the period. Flagged rows are not compared anywhere in this report, and no row is ranked.

## Limits

- **IV is a flat proxy**: DVOL x K (K = 0.8811), the same for every strike and both expiries, so skew and term structure are ignored. The earlier real-IV re-check (`strangle_final_5y_realiv.md`) found that rules positive on this proxy were negative with real archived IV over the last 13 months, so positive numbers here can overstate the real result.
- **Pricing reads DVOL as the harness does**: the copied pricing marks options with the DVOL row stamped at or before each mark time, and a row is the close of the hour that starts at that stamp, so marks can use a DVOL value up to one hour newer than the mark time. This affects option marks only, not the gate inputs (those use DVOL candles that had closed by te). It was kept so the trade model matches `strangle_final_5y.ts`.
- **One path**: five years of one BTC history. Gates were defined beforehand, but TRAIN/VALID/HOLDOUT are still one sequence of markets. The SE is clustered by roll, which handles cohorts that share an exit, but volatility regimes persist across neighbouring rolls, so the SE still understates the true uncertainty.
- **Not backtestable, excluded**: the event calendar (macro stress) and GEX. Any gate that uses them live will behave differently from what is shown.
- **Roll skip**: entries with fewer than 12 bars (3 hours) to the 12:00 UTC roll are skipped (UTC 10:00 and 11:00). Hold length depends on entry hour (from 12 bars at 09:00 UTC to 96 at 12:00 UTC).
- **DVOL holes drop entries silently**: the DVOL cache has 35 holes of 200 hours each, 7,000 missing hours in total, from 2021-12-09 to 2026-08-05, about one every 50 days (median spacing between holes). The cause: `fetch-5y.ts` requests DVOL in 50-day chunks (1,200 hourly candles), the API returns about 1,000 candles per call, and so part of each chunk is never fetched. The data was not re-fetched. During a hole the price path has no IV, so 6,232 of 39,292 entry hours (15.9%) have no Next-expiry price path. They drop out of Entries, Rolls and every statistic, without appearing anywhere else. Only 35 entries with a valid path have no gate DVOL (DVOL as of te is older than 6h), and those land in "Unavail". The holes follow the calendar (fetch chunk boundaries), not market conditions, so they remove the same hours for every gate. The same holes exist in `strangle_final_5y.ts` and in its K calibration.
- **Unavailable inputs**: early in TRAIN the regime state needs 60 completed UTC days of RV; those entries fail every gate that needs ADX or killer and are counted in "Unavail".
- **K overlaps HOLDOUT**: K is calibrated on 2025-10-05 to 2026-10-17, which overlaps HOLDOUT. The IV-based gates (IV/RV) are therefore slightly fitted to the holdout. K is the same constant for every gate.
- **Spreads and fees are assumed** (1.2% half-spread, min $1), not recorded quotes.
